# Data-Analytics-portfolio

End-to-end data analytics portfolio showcasing SQL, Excel, Power BI, PostgreSQL, and Python projects focused on business insights, user behavior, experimentation, customer value, and decision-making.

## Projects

### [A/B Testing: Digital Advertising Campaign](ab-testing-digital-ad-campaign)

A PostgreSQL and Python A/B testing project measuring whether a digital ad campaign improved conversion versus a PSA control group.

**Tools:** PostgreSQL, Python, pandas, statsmodels, matplotlib, seaborn

**Main takeaway:**  
The ad group converted at **2.55%** vs **1.79%** for PSA, producing a **43.09% relative lift** with **p < 0.001**.

---

### [Olist Customer Value & Retention Analysis](olist-customer-value-retention-analysis)

Customer value and retention analysis using the Brazilian Olist e-commerce dataset. This project focuses on customer lifetime value, cohort behavior, customer segmentation, and business recommendations for retention strategy.

**Tools:** PostgreSQL, SQL, Excel, Power BI

**Main takeaway:**  
Olist is heavily acquisition-driven, with most revenue generated from first purchases. The biggest opportunity is improving second-purchase conversion and protecting high-value customers.

---

### [Funnel Analysis Project](funnel-analysis-project)

E-commerce funnel analysis identifying where users drop off across the purchase journey, from page view to purchase.

**Tools:** SQL, Excel

**Main takeaway:**  
The largest opportunity is improving early-stage engagement and product-page conversion, since later checkout stages perform relatively well.

---

### [European Economic Risk Intelligence Platform](european-economic-risk-intelligence-platform)

Economic risk intelligence project analyzing European macroeconomic indicators and country-level risk patterns.

**Tools:** Python, pandas, data visualization

**Main takeaway:**  
The project highlights differences in economic risk across European countries and supports structured comparison of macroeconomic indicators.
